Attendance Settings

These rules decide how punches become Present / Late / Off

Default Staff Schedule

Applies to every teacher who doesn't have a custom schedule below. A staff member is marked Late when their biometric in-time is after the "Late after" time.

Salary Deduction Rules

How attendance turns into salary deductions. Per-day rate = base salary ÷ days in the month.

Per-Teacher Schedules

Override the default for individual teachers (e.g. someone working 11:00–12:30). Leave a field blank to use the default. Blank rows follow the defaults above.

Extra Off Days

Mark specific dates as staff off-days (on top of recurring weekly-offs and whatever the biometric file marked). Applies to all staff.

Threshold Overrides

Different late / half-day cutoffs for a specific weekday (e.g. Saturdays leave early), a date range (e.g. exam week), and/or one teacher. Leave a field blank to keep the normal cutoff. Most specific rule wins.

Upload Attendance

Import the biometric export. Teachers see their own record in the "My Attendance" tab of their portal.

Upload attendance file

Export the biometric All Punch Employee List as CSV. Dates are auto-read from the file header; adjust the period above if needed. Emp Code is matched to each teacher's Employee Code. Punches are read as first = in, last = out. If only an out punch exists (or the first punch falls near the end of the shift), the in punch is recorded as missed and no Late is applied. Late and off-days follow the rules in Attendance Settings.
Re-applies late thresholds and off-days to already-imported data for the period above — no CSV needed. Run this after changing a schedule or weekly-off setting.
